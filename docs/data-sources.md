# Data Sources & Carbon Accounting Standards

EcoTrace relies exclusively on peer-reviewed, internationally audited carbon accounting and lifecycle inventory datasets.

---

## 1. Ecoinvent v3.10
* **Scope:** Global lifecycle inventories covering over 20,000 human activities.
* **Usage in EcoTrace:**
  * Background inventory coefficients for semiconductor lithography, Hall-Héroult aluminum smelting, ethylene petrochemical synthesis, and lithium brine evaporation.
  * Water stress and depletion multipliers using regional water stress basin factors (AWARE methodology).

---

## 3. GLEC Framework v3.0 (Global Logistics Emissions Council)
* **Scope:** The universally accepted standard for calculating greenhouse gas emissions in multi-modal freight transport chains (ISO 14083 aligned).
* **Usage in EcoTrace:**
  * Well-to-Wheel (WTW) factors per tonne-kilometer (tkm) across maritime container shipping, heavy electric rail, chemical parcel tankers, and long-range air cargo.

---

## 4. US EPA WARM (Waste Reduction Model)
* **Scope:** Lifecycle GHG and energy impacts of baseline versus alternative waste management practices.
* **Usage in EcoTrace:**
  * End-of-life landfill leachate factors and recycling displacement credits for engineering polymers, technical fabrics, and e-waste circuit boards.
